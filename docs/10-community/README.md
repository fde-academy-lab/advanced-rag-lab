# 10 · Community

Discussions are not a comment section bolted onto the repo. They are where the exercises
actually run: briefs are posted there, solutions are submitted there, approaches are argued
there, and the accepted answer is the artefact everyone else reads later.

| File | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [discussions-guide.md](discussions-guide.md) | Every category, every label, the twenty-two plays, moderation, the bots, search recipes, and what GitHub will not let you automate |
| [exercise-workflow.md](exercise-workflow.md) | Brief → attempt → submission → peer review → accepted answer, with the rubric at each step |
| [personas.md](personas.md) | The named participants in seeded threads, and why they are labelled |
| [hands-on-roadmap.md](hands-on-roadmap.md) | What the hands-on surfaces do, what is proposed next with the mechanism and the cost, and the three ideas worth arguing about |

**Seeded threads are worked examples.** Every one carries a footer saying so. They exist to
show what a good question, a good wrong turn and a good correction look like, so that the
first real cohort has a standard to write against rather than an empty forum.
