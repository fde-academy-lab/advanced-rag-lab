## How to submit

1. Find the exercise thread in **Exercises & Submissions**.
2. Reply with your approach *before* your code. Approach-first is the habit being trained.
3. Post your result with the number, the interval and the slice.
4. Review one peer's submission before asking for a review of yours.

A clean negative result with a mechanism scores full marks. "It didn't work" without one
scores nothing, and that gap is the entire lesson.
