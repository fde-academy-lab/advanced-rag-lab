# How to run the L.A.B. Simulator from a thread

**Takeaway:** you do not need to clone anything. Post a unit, a bot grades it on a clean
checkout, and the reply names the checks that failed and what to work on.

1. Open [New discussion, LAB Simulator](https://github.com/fde-academy-lab/advanced-rag-lab/discussions/new?category=lab-simulator).
2. Pick a unit from the dropdown. First evening: a drill, `FD1`, `RD1`, `ED1` or `CD1`; they take
   under fifteen minutes each.
3. Write your **approach** first. The form asks for it before the code, and that order is the
   point.
4. Paste your `solution.py` (or, for an `answer` drill such as `RD2`, your filled `answer.yaml`)
   in the code field. Post.
5. Wait about two minutes. The bot replies with named checks, a `teaches` line, what to work on
   for each failed check, what to read, and what is unlocked next.
6. Fix and edit your post; it re-grades. Comment `/hint` for the next hint, `/why <check>` for
   what one check guards, `/status` for the pathway, `/progress` for your own picture.

**Done when:** the thread carries the `cleared` label and `/solution` opens the worked answer.

**If nothing replies:** the post is outside the LAB Simulator category, or the workflow is
disabled. The category name is the only thing the router checks. Ask in
[Q&A](https://github.com/fde-academy-lab/advanced-rag-lab/discussions/categories/q-a).
